Brian Kelly Masonry

A Philadelphia area contractor devoted to building and restoring stone and brick structures.

About us

Brian Kelly Masonry has a high level of craftmanship and regard for the history and methods of the world’s oldest trade. The Philadelphia area has a long and storied history of masonry construction dating back to the 18th century, and our overall vision is focused through this lens.

Eschewing the modern approach of thin stone or manufactured concrete products, we prefer handmade brick, marble, granite, limestone, and building stone which is germane to specific areas, such as Wissahickon schist.

We concentrate on building natural stone and brick walls, fronts, pillars, paving and walkways, as well as repointing and repairs using historically approved mixes that mitigate water damage and preserve masonry structures.

Ultimately, we would like to foster relationships with our customers and the community through high quality work that has a timeless, natural appeal.

We also believe that effective communication and transparency are paramount, and we, likewise, strive to make any consturction project flow as smoothly as possible for both the customer and neighbors.
